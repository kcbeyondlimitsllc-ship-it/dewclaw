How Dewclaw calculates a care plan

Every number in Dewclaw and on its breed guide pages is computed by one care engine from a table of breed data. Nothing is written by hand per breed, and nothing is generated by an AI model. This page says exactly how.

Last reviewed November 10, 2026 · Responsible: KC Beyond Limits LLC, Oregon · kcbeyondlimits.com

Daily calories

Resting energy
RER = 70 × (ideal weight in kg)0.75The standard allometric formula in the WSAVA Global Nutrition Guidelines.
Ideal weight
From the body condition score: each point above or below 5 is treated as about 10% over or under ideal weight.
Life-stage factor
RER is multiplied by a factor for neuter status and age: higher for growing animals and intact adults, lower for neutered adults, seniors and weight loss.
Breed energy and activity
The result is adjusted for the breed's energy level (low ×0.85, moderate ×1, high ×1.15, very high ×1.3) and the activity you choose (low ×0.9, normal ×1, active ×1.15, working ×1.35).
Cups
Calories divided by the calories per cup of your food. Guide pages assume 360 kcal per cup; the calculators let you enter your own.
Treats
10% of daily calories, subtracted from meals.

Weight, life stage and lifespan

Healthy weight range
The adult range from the breed standard, stored in kilograms and shown in pounds.
Life stages
Follow the AAHA canine and AAHA/AAFP feline life stage guidelines, scaled by size: growth ends at 9 months (toy), 10 months (small), 1 yr (medium), 1.33 yr (large), 1.75 yr (giant), 1 yr (cat); senior care starts at 11 (toy), 10 (small), 9 (medium), 7.5 (large), 6 (giant), 11 (cat).
Lifespan
The typical published range for the breed. An individual animal can fall outside it.

Exercise, grooming and health watchlist

Exercise
Minutes a day by breed energy level: low 20–30, moderate 45–60, high 60–90, very high 90–120. Reduced for puppies, seniors and overweight animals.
Grooming
Brushing and bathing cadence by coat type.
Health watchlist
Up to five conditions each breed is known to be predisposed to, with a plain note on what to watch or screen for. A predisposition is not a diagnosis, and the list is not complete.

What this cannot tell you

These are general-population figures. They do not know your pet's medical history, medication, pregnancy, or the food you actually feed. No veterinarian has reviewed an individual plan. Use the numbers to start a conversation with your own vet, and re-check them against your pet's body condition every month.

Sources

  • WSAVA Global Nutrition Guidelines, including the 9-point body condition score.
  • AAHA Canine Life Stage Guidelines and AAHA/AAFP Feline Life Stage Guidelines.
  • Breed standards for adult weight ranges.
  • ASPCA Animal Poison Control for the poison guidance in the app's first-aid cards.

Found a number that looks wrong? Tell us through kcbeyondlimits.com and we will check it against the source.

Dewclaw is not a veterinarian. These figures are computed from published general-population formulas and breed tendencies. They are a starting point for a conversation with your vet, not a diagnosis or a treatment plan. Health conditions, medication, pregnancy and nursing all change them. If something seems wrong with your pet, call your veterinarian or an emergency clinic.
