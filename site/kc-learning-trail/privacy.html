KC's Learning Trail: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026. Published by KC Beyond Limits LLC, Oregon, USA.

The short version

KC's Learning Trail collects no information from you or your child. Nothing leaves the device.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or share any personal information.
  • It has no accounts, no sign-in and no forms.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It has no in-app purchases.
  • It contains no analytics, tracking or third-party software kits.
  • It has no links to websites, stores or other apps, and no social features.
  • The Android app does not ask for internet access or any other device permission.

What stays on the device

The app saves your child's progress (levels, stickers earned and sound settings) on the device so it is there next time. We cannot see it. A grown-up can erase it from the Grown-ups corner inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Spoken instructions are read aloud by the voice already installed on the device.

Children's privacy

The app is made for children aged 3 to 6. Because it collects nothing, there is no personal information from children for us to hold, use or disclose.

Buying the app

The purchase is handled by the store you bought it from, under that store's own privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, we will update this page and its date before that version is released.
