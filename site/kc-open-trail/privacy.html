Privacy policy

KC: Open Trail is a game made by KC Beyond Limits LLC, an Oregon company. It is made for families, and children play it. The short version: the game has no accounts, keeps your save on your own device, shows no adverts, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells data. The rest of this page says exactly what leaves your device, when, and why.

What stays on your device

  • Your save: where KC has been, bones and gems, what KC owns and wears, settings, and the list of purchases made on that device.
  • A random install number the game makes for itself. It is not your name, your phone number or an advertising ID.

All of this is kept in the game's own storage in your browser or app. We cannot see it and we have no copy. Clearing the browser's data for this site, or uninstalling the app, deletes it. Because there is no account, we cannot bring a deleted save back.

What we never ask for

The game does not ask for a name, an email address, a birthday, a photo of you, your contacts, your location, the camera or the microphone. A child can play the whole game without telling us anything.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Loading the game. The game's files come from our website, which is hosted by Netlify. Like any website, the host sees your device's internet (IP) address while it sends the files.
  • Buying something on the web (Stripe). The real-money shop is off until a grown-up turns it on. When a grown-up buys something, the game sends our server three things: which item was chosen, the random install number, and the Trail Key if there is one. Payment then happens on Stripe's own page. The card details, name, email address and billing address go to Stripe, not to the game, and are handled under Stripe's privacy policy. The game never sees them.
  • Buying something in the Google Play app. If the app from Google Play offers purchases, they are sold and billed by Google Play under Google's own terms. We never see your payment details. To confirm a purchase the game sends our server the item, the random install number, the Trail Key if there is one, and the purchase token Google gave it, and our server checks that token with Google Play.
  • Restoring purchases. When a grown-up types a Trail Key to restore permanent items, the game sends that key and the install number to our server.
  • The real-gear shelf. The game shows some real KC Beyond Limits products. Their pictures and prices are part of the game's own files. Nothing is sent to the shop unless a grown-up chooses "See it on the shop", which opens the shop's website. That is a separate website with its own privacy policy, and it may use cookies or tracking of its own.

Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent at any other time.

What our server keeps

Our server runs on Cloudflare. It keeps only what it needs to hand over what was paid for, and to stop a purchase being used twice:

  • For each purchase: the item, the time, and a scrambled (hashed) form of the install number, filed under a scrambled form of the payment's reference. Web purchase records are kept for about 400 days. Google Play purchase records are kept until you ask us to delete them.
  • For each Trail Key: the list of permanent items bought with it. Kept until you ask us to delete it.
  • For restore attempts: a counter for the day, filed under a scrambled form of the internet address the attempt came from, so that nobody can guess keys. Kept for two days.

Stripe and Google Play keep their own records of payments under their own policies. We can see those payment records (for example the email address typed on Stripe's page) in order to give refunds and answer questions.

We do not show adverts, use analytics or advertising identifiers, fingerprint devices, build profiles, sell or rent data, or share anything with data brokers.

Children and grown-ups

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Playing the game sends us nothing about the player.
  • Buying is for grown-ups. The shop is off on every device until a grown-up turns it on, and the game asks a grown-up question before each purchase and before any link that leaves the game. That question is a speed bump for a child. It is not proof of age, and it is not a parental-consent check.
  • If you believe a child has given us or a payment company personal information through the game, or bought something without permission, contact us and we will delete what we hold and sort out a refund.

Your choices

  • Play without ever turning the shop on. Nothing is sent to our server.
  • Delete your save at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser or uninstalling the app.
  • Ask us to delete a Trail Key or a purchase record from our server. Tell us the Trail Key, or the date and amount of the purchase, and we will remove it within 30 days. Items already restored on a device stay on that device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over personal data, such as asking for a copy or for correction. Contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new date. We will not start collecting more than this page describes without changing this page first.
