How many calories does a dog need?

Start from the resting energy requirement, RER = 70 × (weight in kg)0.75, then multiply by a factor for life stage: about 1.6 for a neutered adult dog and 1.8 for an intact one, more for a growing puppy, less for weight loss. Breed energy and activity move it from there.

Getting the portion right

Feed the target weight
Calculate from the weight your dog should be. Feeding an overweight dog for its current weight keeps it overweight.
Read the bag
Calories per cup range from under 300 to over 450 between foods. The figure is on the label as kcal/cup or kcal/kg.
Weigh, do not scoop
Cup measures run 20–30% off. A kitchen scale and the kcal/kg figure are more accurate.
Count the treats
Keep treats to 10% of the day and take them out of the meal total.
Re-check monthly
A calculator gives a starting point. Adjust by body condition score, about 10% at a time.
