Body condition score chart for dogs and cats

A scale tells you what your pet weighs. Body condition tells you whether that weight is right for their frame. Vets score it from 1 to 9, and 4 to 5 is ideal. You can do it at home with your hands in about a minute.

Last reviewed November 10, 2026 · Based on the WSAVA 9-point body condition score

How to check

Ribs
Run your fingers along the ribcage with light pressure. You should feel each rib under a thin cover, like the back of your hand.
Waist
Look from above. There should be a clear narrowing behind the ribs.
Tuck
Look from the side. The belly should rise from the chest toward the hind legs.

The nine scores

ScoreBandWhat you see and feel
1Too thinRibs, spine and hip bones visible from a distance. No fat, obvious muscle loss.
2Too thinRibs easily visible, bony landmarks prominent, minimal muscle over them.
3Too thinRibs visible without fat cover, waist and abdominal tuck obvious, tops of spine visible.
4IdealRibs easily felt with a thin fat cover. Waist clear from above, abdomen tucked from the side.
5IdealRibs felt without pressing. Waist visible behind the ribs, abdomen tucked. This is the target.
6OverweightRibs felt under a slight excess of fat. Waist visible from above but not sharply.
7OverweightRibs hard to feel under heavy fat. No waist from above, only a slight abdominal tuck.
8ObeseRibs cannot be felt. Obvious fat over the spine and tail base, no waist, abdomen distended.
9ObeseHeavy fat over the ribs, spine and tail base. Fat on the neck and limbs, obvious distension.

What to do with the score

1 to 3
See your vet before changing food. Unexplained thinness needs a cause, not just more calories.
4 to 5
Hold the current portion, weigh monthly and re-score when the seasons or the food change.
6 to 7
Each point over 5 is roughly 10% over ideal weight. Feed for the target weight, cut treats to 10% of the day and re-score monthly.
8 to 9
Ask your vet for a weight-loss plan. Cutting food hard on your own is risky, especially for cats, who can develop liver disease if they stop eating.
