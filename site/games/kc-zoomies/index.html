How to play

  • Run: hold the arrow buttons, or ← → / A D. Downhill builds speed and carries you up the next rise.
  • Jump: the JUMP button, or Space / ↑ / W. Hold it to jump higher.
  • Zoomies: collect KC's orange-and-blue balls to fill the meter, then zoom through brambles at top speed.
  • Paws: brambles, pine cones and the grumpy goose cost a paw. Mud and puddles slow you down. Splash in the river and you start again from the last trail sign.
  • Golden bones: three hide off the trail in every level. Mushrooms and log ramps help you reach them.
  • Pause: the pause button, P or Esc. R restarts the level, M mutes.
