Dewclaw · Privacy policy · Effective October 19, 2026

Privacy policy

Dewclaw is made by KC Beyond Limits LLC, an Oregon company. The short version: Dewclaw has no accounts, keeps your pets' information on your own device, shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking, and never sells data. The rest of this page says exactly what leaves your device, when, and why.

What stays on your device

  • Your pets (name, breed, age, weight, body condition, activity level), their charts, health records and reminders, walk and potty logs, training progress, sitter sheets, lost-pet poster details and photos.
  • Your Geopet, its stamps and bandanas.
  • Your settings, including the area you chose for the Nearby screen.

All of this is stored in the app's own storage on your phone. We cannot see it. Uninstalling Dewclaw deletes it; the Settings screen and the Geopet "Start over" button clear parts of it sooner.

What leaves your device, and to whom

  • Maps and places (Nearby, Geopet). To show dog parks, trails, daycares, vets, groomers and events, the app asks OpenStreetMap services (the Overpass and Nominatim servers) for places near the area you chose. Your area is rounded to roughly 11 km before it is sent. Map images come from OpenStreetMap tile servers. These services receive your device's IP address, as any website would.
  • Location. The GPS walk tracker and Geopet check-ins use your precise location only while you use them, and only on the device: distance is measured on your phone, and a check-in compares your position to the stop locally. Your precise location is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can refuse the location permission and still use everything except walk tracking and check-ins.
  • DewclawMD research. After the built-in guide answers, DewclawMD looks the question up in free public databases: Europe PMC (peer-reviewed literature), openFDA (the FDA's open data) and Wikipedia. The text of your question, and the breed and species of the pet it concerns, are sent to those services so they can search. Turn "Look things up online" off in Settings and nothing is sent.
  • DewclawMD for Plus subscribers. When Dewclaw Plus is on and you ask DewclawMD a question, the question and a short text summary of the pet's chart (species, breed, age, weight, body condition, and the chart's figures) are sent to our Dewclaw server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude model to write the answer. We do not store your questions or answers after the answer is returned. Anthropic's handling of API data is described in Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • Dewclaw Plus purchases. Plus is sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. To confirm a purchase and to count your monthly DewclawMD allowance, the app sends Google's purchase token for your subscription to our Dewclaw server, which checks it with Google Play. We keep that token, its expiry date and a monthly question count, and nothing else about you.
We do not run ads, use analytics or advertising identifiers, fingerprint devices, sell or rent data, or share anything with data brokers.

Children

Dewclaw is for pet owners and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Your choices

  • Use Dewclaw without the location permission, and without the online research switch.
  • Delete your pets' data at any time by deleting the pet, using "Start over" in Geopet, or uninstalling the app.
  • Cancel Dewclaw Plus at any time in Google Play. To have the server forget your purchase token and question count, email us the Google order number and we will remove them within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Changes that affect what leaves your device will also be noted in the app's Settings screen.
