Dewclaw › For shelters and rescues

A free care plan for every adopter

The first weeks after adoption are when new owners have the most questions and the least idea who to ask. Dewclaw gives each adopter a care plan for the animal they just took home. It costs your organization nothing and there is nothing for you to run.

What an adopter gets

A feeding plan
Daily calories and cups for their pet's breed, age and weight, with a treat budget.
A vaccine and checkup schedule
What is due and when, with reminders on their phone.
First-aid cards
The first minutes of common emergencies and the poison-control numbers. They work with no signal.
A lost-pet poster and a sitter sheet
Ready to print or share in a minute.
Breed guides
Healthy weight, exercise and the health problems to watch for, for 140 dog and 41 cat breeds, including mixed breeds by size.

Why it suits an adoption packet

Free for the adopter
Everything above is free. There is an optional paid tier called Plus; nobody needs it to use the care plan, first aid or reminders.
No account and no data collection
Adopters do not sign up. Their pet's details stay on their own phone. Dewclaw shows no ads. See the privacy policy.
Nothing to install
It opens in the phone's browser from a link or a QR code and can be added to the home screen.
Not a substitute for a vet
Every screen says so, and the plan points adopters to a veterinarian for anything medical.

How to use it

Simplest
Put this link in your adoption email or packet: dewclaw-app.netlify.app
Better
Link straight to the animal's breed so the chart opens filled in. Every breed page has a "Build the chart" button; copy that link.
A printed card with a QR code
A printable card is available on request. Write to us through kcbeyondlimits.com.

If something seems wrong with your pet, call your veterinarian or an emergency clinic.
