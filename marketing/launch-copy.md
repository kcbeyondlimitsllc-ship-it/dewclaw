# Launch copy

Ready to paste. Links use `dewclaw-app.netlify.app`; swap in the new address if you move to your own domain first. Nothing here has been posted or sent.

## 1. Tester call (for the Play closed test)

Post on Instagram, Facebook and the YouTube community tab. Pin it.

```
I built an app for KC and I need 12 of you to help me get it onto Google Play.

It's called Dewclaw. You tell it your pet's breed, age and weight and it works out how much to feed them, how far to walk them and what's due at the vet.

Google makes new developers run a 14-day test with 12 people before an app can go public. If you have an Android phone and two minutes, comment TESTER and I'll send you the link.

No account, no ads. You just have to keep it installed for two weeks.
```

## 2. YouTube Short / TikTok / Reel: "How much should KC actually eat?"

**Viewer promise:** find out in 30 seconds whether you are overfeeding your dog.

**Title (recommended):** I was overfeeding my dog. Here's the real number.

Alternatives:
- How much should your dog actually eat? (30-second check)
- The dog food bag is lying to you

**Script** (shots are suggestions; use whatever footage you have of KC at mealtime):

| Time | On screen | Voice |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | KC staring at her bowl. Text: "Am I overfeeding her?" | "The bag says three cups. The bag is wrong for most dogs." |
| 3 to 12 s | Screen recording: pick KC's breed, type her weight and age | "I put in her breed, her age and her weight." |
| 12 to 22 s | The calories and cups cards. Text: KC's real number | "This is what she actually needs in a day. And treats come out of that, not on top." |
| 22 to 30 s | KC eating the measured portion. Text: "Link in bio. No sign-up." | "It's free, there's no account. Try your dog. Link's in my bio." |

Record KC's real numbers from the app before filming so the on-screen figure is true. Do not change her food for the video.

**Description:**

```
How much should your dog eat a day? I ran KC's breed, age and weight through Dewclaw, the app I built for her, and the answer was not what the bag said.

Try your own dog (no account): https://dewclaw-app.netlify.app/dog-calorie-calculator/

Dewclaw is not a veterinarian. Ask yours before changing your dog's diet.

#dogfood #dogcare #doghealth #dogtok #pnwdogs
```

**Pinned comment:**

```
What breed is your dog? Tell me and I'll reply with their healthy weight range.
```

Replying with a number from the breed pages gives you a reason to answer every comment, which is the part that builds the community.

Judge these on cost per person who opens the calculator, not on likes. Stop any variant that has spent $15 without beating the others.
